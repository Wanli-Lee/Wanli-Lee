I'm a PhD student at the **State Key Laboratory of CAD&CG, Zhejiang University**, and a research intern with the **Qwen Team at Alibaba**. Previously, I interned at **Microsoft Research Asia** and **BAAI**.

My research focuses on **agentic foundation models**, especially **computer-use agents** and **Agentic RL** algorithms and training infrastructure. I aim to build agents that learn and act reliably in unfamiliar real-world environments. I'm also interested in **multimodal generation and alignment**.
